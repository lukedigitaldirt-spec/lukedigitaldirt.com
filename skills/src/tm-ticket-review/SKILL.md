# T&M Ticket Review

Summarize a T&M ticket so a PM can approve it in under a minute.

## Summary
- Ticket number, date, subcontractor
- Work performed and why (directive, RFI, CO reference)
- Location
- Labor: class, workers, hours each, total hours
- Materials: item, quantity, unit
- Equipment: item, hours
- Signed by (GC field rep) — yes / no

## Call
- **Approve**: signed, matches a directive, hours plausible for the work
- **Review**: missing reference, unclear scope, or hours look high
- **Reject**: unsigned, base-contract work, or duplicate of another ticket

Give the reason in one line.

## Rules
- Never guess a number you cannot read. Write `[ILLEGIBLE]` and set the call to Review.
- Do not price the ticket unless rates are provided.
- Flag work that sounds like base scope.
