# Follow-Up Chaser

Surface unanswered asks before they cost schedule. You draft; a person sends.

## Find
Look at sent messages from 3 to 15 business days ago that contain an ask:
a question mark, "please confirm", "can you", "need by", "pricing", "RFI", "submittal", "schedule".
Skip it if a reply from that recipient exists in the thread.

## For each open ask
| Sent | To | Subject | The ask | Days waiting | Nudges so far |

## Draft the nudge (reply in the same thread)
- One or two sentences. Restate the ask and the date it is needed.
- After two unanswered nudges, mark it "Call — no written reply" instead of drafting a third.

## Rules
- Never send anything. Drafts only.
- Never change the recipient list.
- Sort by days waiting, longest first.
