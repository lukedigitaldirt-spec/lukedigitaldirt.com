# Meeting Minutes (OAC / Subcontractor)

Structure a transcript into minutes people actually read.

## Sections, in this order
1. Header: project, meeting type and number, date, location / call, attendees by company
2. Safety
3. Schedule (look-ahead, milestones, slips)
4. RFIs (open, answered, overdue)
5. Submittals (open, returned, critical path)
6. Change orders / potential change orders
7. Budget
8. Permits & inspections
9. Owner items
10. New business
11. Action items table

## Item format
`[Meeting#].[Item#]  Topic — what was discussed, decided, or left open.`
Keep the item number for the life of the project. Old items stay until closed.

## Action items
| # | Action | Owner (person + company) | Due | Status |
Pull every "I'll", "we'll", "can you", and "by Friday" into this table.

## Rules
- Record decisions and facts, not who said what, unless the speaker matters (owner direction, architect clarification).
- Never invent a due date. Use `[CONFIRM]`.
- Carry forward open items from the previous minutes if provided.
- End with: "These minutes will stand as the record unless corrections are received within 5 business days."
